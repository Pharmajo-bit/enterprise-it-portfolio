# Service Desk and IT Operations

Hands-on projects covering service-desk platforms, ticket lifecycle management, L1/L2 troubleshooting, escalation, service-level agreements and knowledge management.

## Projects

| Project | Focus |
|---|---|
| [osTicket service desk operations lab](osticket-service-desk-lab/) | Docker deployment, troubleshooting, RBAC, realistic support workflows, escalation, SLAs and internal knowledge documentation |
| [ServiceNow service desk lab](servicenow-service-desk-lab/) | Enterprise incident management, assignment groups, group-based access, L1/L2 escalation, SLA breach handling, knowledge and analytics |

## Platform progression

The osTicket project demonstrates deployment and operational troubleshooting in a self-hosted platform. The ServiceNow project transfers the strongest workflows into an enterprise ITSM environment and adds structured incident records, Task SLAs, knowledge publishing and Platform Analytics.
